Privacy and Beta signup

AirNudger is an independent Android notification companion developed by DRS Studio in Montréal, Canada.

Beta signup emails

When you submit the Beta signup form, we collect the email address you enter and the time of your submission. DRS Studio uses that information to contact you about AirNudger Beta access and Beta updates. Joining does not guarantee immediate access.

The form is hosted by Google Forms and responses are saved in a private Google spreadsheet in our account. The signup list is not published on the website or shared with other respondents. Google processes form submissions and connection information to provide its service. The embedded form may use Google cookies or your existing Google session; you do not need a Google account to submit your email.

We do not sell the signup list. To leave the list or request deletion of your signup information, email support@airnudger.com.

Website hosting

The website runs on Cloudflare Pages. Its own code does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, connection details and request logs to deliver and protect the website. Loading the signup form also connects your browser to Google.

Support email

Messages sent to support@airnudger.com are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to DRS Studio’s Gmail inbox. The support providers process the message to route and deliver it. We use the information you send to respond to your request.

About the Android app

AirNudger is in Beta. Android notification access is a broad system permission; the app filters relayed alerts by selected apps and notification types. Optional direct phone-call detection requests Phone-state permission. These website and signup details do not replace the app’s own permission information and privacy disclosures.

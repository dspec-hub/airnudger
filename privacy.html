Privacy and Beta signup

AirNudger is an independent Android notification companion developed by DRS Studio in Montréal, Canada.

Beta signup emails

When you submit the Beta signup form, we collect the email address you enter and the time of your submission. DRS Studio uses that information to contact you about AirNudger Beta access and Beta updates. Joining does not guarantee immediate access.

The form is hosted by Google Forms and responses are saved in a private Google spreadsheet in our account. The signup list is not published on the website or shared with other respondents. Google processes form submissions and connection information to provide its service. The embedded form may use Google cookies or your existing Google session; you do not need a Google account to submit your email.

We do not sell the signup list. To leave the list or request deletion of your signup information, email support@airnudger.com.

Website hosting

The website runs on Cloudflare Pages. We use Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Until then, our analytics code does not load Google Tag Manager or send Analytics measurements. We do not install advertising pixels. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, connection details and request logs to deliver and protect the website. Loading the signup form also connects your browser to Google.

Optional website analytics

If you allow analytics, Google receives usage information such as page visits, scrolling, outbound links, downloads, browser/device information and approximate location derived from connection information. Analytics cookies can recognise repeat visits. We use this to understand how people find and use the website. Advertising storage, ad personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this setup. Beta signup email addresses and message content are not sent to Analytics, and automatic form-interaction measurement is disabled.

Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. Use to change it. Declining or withdrawing does not affect access to the website or Beta signup. Withdrawal stops further Analytics loading and clears Analytics cookies accessible to this site; it does not erase information already processed by Google. Google Forms and Cloudflare operate separately as described above.

Learn more in Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Support email

Messages sent to support@airnudger.com are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to DRS Studio’s Gmail inbox. The support providers process the message to route and deliver it. We use the information you send to respond to your request.

About the Android app

AirNudger is in Beta. Android notification access is a broad system permission; the app filters relayed alerts by selected apps and notification types. Optional direct phone-call detection requests Phone-state permission. These website and signup details do not replace the app’s own permission information and privacy disclosures.

DOES FITBIT AIR HAVE PHONE NOTIFICATIONS?

A screenless tracker.
A missing phone alert.

Fitbit Air’s native Google Health setup does not mirror ordinary incoming calls, texts and app notifications to your wrist. Its tracker alarms and health features are different from Android phone notifications.

AirNudger is being developed for people who want optional vibration alerts on Fitbit Air without wearing a smartwatch or adding another screen. Choose the Android notifications you want to feel, rather than turning every app into an interruption.

See the questions owners are asking in Google Health Community and r/fitbit. These discussions describe the need; they are not endorsements or compatibility tests of AirNudger.

ANDROID CALLS, TEXTS & APP ALERTS

AirNudger relays selected notifications from your nearby Android phone over Bluetooth. Feel a short signal for an incoming call, message or reminder, then check your phone when it suits you.

01 / CALLS & MESSAGES

Incoming call and text alerts

Choose your phone or messaging apps for wrist vibrations. SMS, WhatsApp and other chat alerts depend on the Android notifications those apps post and the rules you select. Fitbit Air does not display messages or caller names.

02 / CUSTOM VIBRATIONS

Recognise the app by its pattern

Assign a vibration preset to each selected app. Try single, double, three-pulse or five-pulse sequences and preview them on your tracker. Short pulses give different alerts a recognisable feel.

03 / FEWER INTERRUPTIONS

Optional alerts and quiet hours

Want calls and texts, without every app buzzing? Select apps and available notification types, set quiet hours or pause the relay. Android silence and Do Not Disturb behaviour should be tested on your phone.

ABOUT US · DRS STUDIO · MONTRÉAL

The alerts you want.
The screen you don’t.

AirNudger is an independent notification companion for Fitbit Air, developed by DRS Studio in Montréal, Canada. The idea is simple: keep a screenless tracker and choose which Android phone notifications deserve a nudge on your wrist.

Built on the open-source fitbit-air project by Thore Koritzius (The Centaurus), AirNudger focuses on selected app alerts, per-app vibration patterns and practical control over interruptions.

Good to know

  • Vibrations usually arrive a few seconds after the notification; timing varies.
  • Your Air must be near your Android phone. Missed alerts are not replayed.
  • AirNudger uses an undocumented Fitbit Air interface. A tracker firmware update can stop it working until AirNudger is updated.
  • Device and firmware compatibility are still being tested. Google Health sync and alarm access currently require the pause, full Sync and resume workflow.

AirNudger and DRS Studio are not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Fitbit.

FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

Calls, texts, vibration settings, Android devices and Google Health: answers to the questions Fitbit Air owners keep asking.

Does Fitbit Air have phone notifications?

Fitbit Air does not provide standard call, text and app notification mirroring through its native Google Health setup. AirNudger is a separate, unofficial Android companion designed to add selected notification vibrations. It does not add a screen or display messages.

Can Fitbit Air vibrate for incoming calls?

AirNudger supports incoming-call vibration alerts from selected Android phone notifications. Optional direct phone-call detection requires Android 12 or newer, one active SIM and a Phone-state permission. It has limited Pixel testing; instant alerts and continuous ringing are not guaranteed.

Can I get text message or WhatsApp notifications on Fitbit Air?

AirNudger can relay selected notifications posted by Android messaging apps, including SMS and WhatsApp, as short wrist vibrations. The app must post a notification that AirNudger can access, and your rules must allow it. Message text is not displayed on Fitbit Air. Compatibility varies by app, phone and tracker firmware.

Why is there no Notifications setting for Fitbit Air in Google Health?

Instructions for Fitbit models with native phone notification support do not necessarily apply to Fitbit Air. A notification permission for Google Health on your phone does not by itself enable call and text vibrations on your Air. AirNudger manages its own alert rules separately.

How do I enable Fitbit Air notification vibrations on Android?

For the current private build, first pair Fitbit Air in Google Health. Grant AirNudger notification access and Nearby devices access through Android settings, select apps and notification types in Rules, then preview a vibration pattern with the tracker nearby. The app is in Beta. Join the Beta list on this page to hear about access and updates; a public download is not available yet.

Does AirNudger work on Google Pixel or Samsung Galaxy phones?

AirNudger is developed for Android devices. Current hands-on testing is limited to a Pixel 9 Pro XL; Samsung Galaxy and other Android phones are not yet verified for this app. Android version, Bluetooth behaviour, background restrictions and Fitbit Air firmware can affect delivery.

Can I choose only calls and texts, instead of every app notification?

Yes. Select which Android apps and available notification types may trigger an alert. Assign a vibration preset per app, set quiet hours or pause the relay. Available categories depend on the notifications supplied by each app.

Can Fitbit Air use different vibration patterns for different apps?

AirNudger offers per-app vibration presets, including single, double, three-pulse and five-pulse sequences. Preview the pattern before saving. These are discrete pulses, not an arbitrary continuous vibration or a display of who is calling.

Will Fitbit Air vibrate when my Android phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb?

Delivery depends on Android notification behaviour and AirNudger’s silence and quiet-hours settings. Do not assume that silent mode and Do Not Disturb behave identically or that AirNudger bypasses them. Check your chosen rules and test the mode you use on your own phone.

Are phone alarms and calendar reminders the same as Fitbit Air alarms?

No. Google Health tracker alarms are separate from notifications posted by Android Clock or calendar apps. AirNudger can only act on an accessible, selected phone notification; it does not copy your phone alarm schedule into Fitbit Air. Test reminders on your setup and do not rely on the app for critical alarms.

Will alerts arrive instantly, and what happens if my phone is out of range?

Vibrations usually arrive a few seconds after the notification, and actual delay varies. Fitbit Air must be near your phone over Bluetooth. Missed alerts are not replayed. Background restrictions, connection state and firmware affect reliability.

Can I keep using Google Health sync and alarms?

Yes, with an explicit handoff in the current app: pause AirNudger notifications, run a full tracker Sync in Google Health, use Alarms, then return to AirNudger and resume. Seamless sharing and overnight behaviour are still being tested.

Does AirNudger work with iPhone?

No. AirNudger is an Android companion app; there is no AirNudger iOS version. The fact that Fitbit Air can pair with an iPhone does not make this Android notification relay available on iPhone.

ANSWERS, WITHOUT THE GUESSWORK

Fitbit Air notification questions

Can Fitbit Air vibrate for phone notifications?

AirNudger is an independent Android companion designed to turn selected phone notifications into vibration alerts on Fitbit Air. It is currently a development app with limited device testing.

Can I get Fitbit Air call alerts?

AirNudger supports notification-based incoming-call alerts. Optional direct phone-call detection requires Android 12 or newer, one active SIM and a Phone-state permission. This experimental option has had one successful Pixel trial; delivery speed and compatibility are not guaranteed.

Can I choose different vibration patterns?

Yes. Choose a vibration preset for each selected app, including single, double, three-pulse and five-pulse sequences. These are short pulses, not continuous ringing.

Does AirNudger work with iPhone?

The current app is for Android. There is no AirNudger iPhone version.

Does it replace Google Health?

No. Pair Fitbit Air through Google Health first. To access Google Health sync and alarms, pause AirNudger, run a full tracker Sync in Google Health, then resume notifications. Seamless sharing is still being tested.

Is AirNudger available to download?

AirNudger is currently in private development. A public download has not been released. This page will be updated when a public build is ready.

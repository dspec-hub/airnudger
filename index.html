DOES FITBIT AIR HAVE PHONE NOTIFICATIONS?

A screenless tracker.
A missing phone alert.

Fitbit Air’s native Google Health setup does not mirror ordinary incoming calls, texts and app notifications to your wrist. Its tracker alarms and health features are different from Android phone notifications.

AirNudger is being developed for people who want optional vibration alerts on Fitbit Air without wearing a smartwatch or adding another screen. Choose the Android notifications you want to feel, rather than turning every app into an interruption.

See the questions owners are asking in Google Health Community and r/fitbit. These discussions describe the need; they are not endorsements or compatibility tests of AirNudger.

AirNudger is an independent notification companion for Fitbit Air, developed by DRS Studio in Montréal, Canada. The idea is simple: keep a screenless tracker and choose which Android phone notifications deserve a nudge on your wrist.

AirNudger focuses on selected app alerts, per-app vibration patterns and practical control over interruptions. Developed by DRS Studio in Montréal, Canada, it is currently available for Beta testing by invitation.

Good to know

  • Vibrations usually arrive a few seconds after the notification; timing varies.
  • Your Air must be near your Android phone. Missed alerts are not replayed.
  • AirNudger uses an undocumented Fitbit Air interface. A tracker firmware update can stop it working until AirNudger is updated.
  • Device and firmware compatibility are still being tested. Google Health sync and alarm access currently require the pause, full Sync and resume workflow.

FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

Calls, texts, vibration settings, Android devices and Google Health: answers to the questions Fitbit Air owners keep asking.

Does Fitbit Air have phone notifications?

Fitbit Air doesn’t normally mirror calls, texts or app notifications through Google Health. AirNudger is a separate Android companion that can turn the notifications you choose into vibrations on your wrist. Your Air won’t display the message or caller’s name.

Can Fitbit Air vibrate for incoming calls?

Yes. With AirNudger, you can choose incoming-call notifications from your Android phone to trigger a vibration. Optional direct call detection needs Android 12 or later, one active SIM and Phone-state permission. It has had limited Pixel testing, so instant alerts and continuous ringing aren’t guaranteed.

Can I get text message or WhatsApp notifications on Fitbit Air?

Yes. You can choose SMS, WhatsApp and other messaging notifications from your Android phone and feel a short vibration on your Fitbit Air. The messaging app needs to post a notification, and your AirNudger settings need to allow it. Your Air won’t show the message. Results can vary by phone, app and tracker firmware.

Why is there no Notifications setting for Fitbit Air in Google Health?

Fitbit Air’s Google Health setup doesn’t offer the same phone notification settings as some other Fitbit models. Giving Google Health notification permission won’t turn on call or text vibrations by itself. AirNudger has its own settings for the alerts you want to feel.

How do I enable Fitbit Air notification vibrations on Android?

First, pair your Fitbit Air with Google Health on your Android phone. In AirNudger, allow notification access and Nearby devices access, choose your apps and alert types, then preview a vibration with your Air nearby. AirNudger is in Beta; join the list on this page to hear about access.

Does AirNudger work on Google Pixel or Samsung Galaxy phones?

AirNudger is made for Android. It has been tested hands-on with a Pixel 9 Pro XL, but Samsung Galaxy and other Android phones haven’t been verified yet. Bluetooth, background settings, Android version and Fitbit Air firmware can all affect how alerts work.

Can I choose only calls and texts, instead of every app notification?

Yes. Pick the apps and available notification types you want, so you can keep calls and texts without every app buzzing your wrist. You can also choose vibration patterns, set quiet hours or pause notifications.

Can Fitbit Air use different vibration patterns for different apps?

Yes. You can give selected apps different vibration presets, including one, two, three or five pulses, and preview them on your Fitbit Air. The pattern helps you recognise an alert, but your Air won’t show who is calling or display a message.

Will Fitbit Air vibrate when my Android phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb?

It depends on your Android phone’s notification settings and the silence and quiet-hours options you choose in AirNudger. Silent mode and Do Not Disturb can behave differently, so try your preferred settings on your own phone rather than assuming alerts will get through.

Are phone alarms and calendar reminders the same as Fitbit Air alarms?

Your Fitbit Air alarms in Google Health are separate from notifications sent by Android Clock or calendar apps. AirNudger can relay a selected phone notification when it can access it, but it won’t copy your phone’s alarm schedule to your tracker. Test your reminders and use a reliable backup for critical alarms.

Will alerts arrive instantly, and what happens if my phone is out of range?

Your Air needs to stay near your phone over Bluetooth. Vibrations usually arrive a few seconds after a notification, but timing varies with your connection, phone settings and firmware. Alerts missed while disconnected aren’t replayed.

Can I keep using Google Health sync and alarms?

Yes. For now, pause AirNudger notifications, run a full tracker Sync in Google Health, and manage your alarms there. Then return to AirNudger and resume notifications. Seamless sharing and overnight behaviour are still being tested.

Does AirNudger work with iPhone?

No. AirNudger is currently an Android app and isn’t available for iPhone. Even if your Fitbit Air pairs with an iPhone, this notification companion requires an Android phone.

Who makes AirNudger, and how do I join the Beta?

AirNudger is developed by DRS Studio in Montréal, Canada. If you’d like to try it, enter your email in the Beta signup form on this page. We’ll contact you about access and updates; joining doesn’t guarantee immediate access. AirNudger is independent and isn’t affiliated with Google or Fitbit. It builds on the open-source fitbit-air project by Thore Koritzius (The Centaurus).
